Pricing FAQ

What counts as a CSM seat?
Anyone who owns accounts, works Playbook tasks or builds EBR decks. Viewer seats, for people who only read scores and dashboards, are free and unlimited on every plan.
What counts as an account?
A customer company you track in Harbor Desk, usually one per CRM account. Contacts, users and subsidiaries inside that company don't count separately.
Is there a free trial?
No. A health score needs your data to mean anything, and a trial without it shows you nothing. Instead, we run a demo on a sample of your accounts and, on Growth and Enterprise, a 30-day paid pilot credited against your first year.
Can we pay monthly?
No. All plans are billed annually, invoiced, with net 30 payment terms. Multi-year agreements are available on Growth and Enterprise.
What happens if we go over our account limit?
Nothing stops working. We'll tell you, and we'll talk about moving plans at your next renewal, not mid-term.
Do you charge for integrations?
No. Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Segment, Slack and our API are included on every plan. Warehouse Sync for Snowflake and BigQuery is the one add-on.
Do you offer discounts?
For nonprofits and for multi-year agreements. Talk to sales.
