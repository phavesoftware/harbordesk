Questions people ask before the call

Do we need to share our data for the demo?
No. We use a sample book of accounts built to match your company size and segment. If you want to see your own data, we can set up a paid pilot after the call: 30 days on Growth or Enterprise, credited against your first year.
Is there a free trial instead?
No. A health score needs your data to mean anything, and a trial without it shows you nothing. The demo is the quickest way to see whether Harbor Desk fits.
How long does it take to go live?
Most teams are live in 30 days, with Health Scores running on their full book of accounts in the first two weeks. Premium Onboarding adds a dedicated implementation lead and is included on Enterprise.
Which systems do you connect to?
Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Segment, Snowflake, BigQuery and Slack. Your CRM stays the system of record.
What does it cost?
Starter is $18,000 a year with 10 CSM seats, Growth is $42,000 a year with 25 CSM seats, and Enterprise starts at $90,000 a year with 50 CSM seats. The pricing page has the details, and we'll give you a straight number on the call.
