How a pilot runs

Four weeks, one question: does it see churn coming on your book?

  1. Week 1: connect and score

    We connect your CRM and one product data source, then back-test a health score against your last four quarters of renewals.

  2. Weeks 2 and 3: act on it

    Your CSMs work from the score, and two Playbooks run on the accounts that turn amber: a usage drop and a sponsor change.

  3. Week 4: decide

    We review which accounts the score flagged and what your team did about them. You decide on the plan, and the pilot fee comes off your first year.
