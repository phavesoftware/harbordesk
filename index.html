The product

Three things, done properly.

Most customer success software tries to be a CRM, a help desk and a survey tool at once. Harbor Desk does three jobs: tell you which accounts are drifting, do something about it, and prove your value to the executive who signs the renewal.

01

Health Scores

A score for every account, built from the signals that actually predict churn in your business: logins, feature adoption, open tickets, sponsor changes and invoice status. Every score shows its working, so a CSM can say why an account is red in one sentence.

02

Playbooks

Automated actions that fire when health changes. A sponsor leaves, a playbook opens a task for the CSM, drafts an intro email to the new contact and flags the account for the weekly risk review. You write the rules once. Harbor Desk runs them on every account, every day.

03

EBR Builder

Executive business review decks assembled from real usage data: adoption trends, outcomes against the goals the customer set, support history and a recommended next step. Your CSM edits the story instead of copying numbers into slides.
