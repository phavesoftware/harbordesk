Where to start

Pick the problem you have this quarter. Each topic below links to the post and the part of Harbor Desk that deals with it.

"Churn keeps surprising us."

Your health score probably can't explain itself, or it was never checked against a real renewal. Start with Building a health score your CSMs trust, then see how Health Scores show their working on every account.

"Our CSMs ignore the alerts."

Too many playbooks, firing on too many accounts, with no clear action. Read Playbooks your CSMs won't ignore for the three we'd start with, and how Playbooks report on their own outcomes.

"Renewals are hard even when the account looks fine."

The executive sponsor may not know what they're getting. Running EBRs that renew covers the five-section review we recommend, and the EBR Builder puts the data together for you.
