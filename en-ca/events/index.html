How our events work

  • Small on purpose. Workshops take 8 companies at most, so every team gets time on its own accounts.
  • Working sessions, not pitches. The product comes up only if you ask. Nobody from sales is on the call.
  • Off the record. We don't record workshops, because teams talk about real accounts. You get a written summary afterwards instead.
  • Free. Customers and non-customers alike.
  • Useful afterwards. Every workshop ends with something written you can take to your next team meeting: for the Churn-risk teardown workshop, a teardown of your churn-risk signals and the three conversations to have first.

Run the workshop for your own team

If none of the dates work, or you'd rather work through your accounts without other companies in the room, we run private sessions of the Churn-risk teardown workshop for one team at a time. Same 60 minutes, same written teardown afterwards, and room for up to six people from your side, which suits a CS leadership team plus its CS ops leads.

Private sessions are free too, and they are still not a sales pitch. We schedule a handful each month, so ask early. To request one, talk to our team and mention the workshop.
